# Source attribution and distinct data terms

The code license does not replace the terms of third-party scientific inputs. Original data, gene definitions and papers retain their respective source rights. No claim of a blanket MIT data license is made.

- CPTAC discovery data. Dou et al., Cell 2020, DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2020.01.026, PMID 32059776. Source route https://zenodo.org/records/8394329. The record API reports CC-BY-4.0; relevant publisher-processed data are subset and gene-reconciled for analysis. Derived phenotype and score designs are identified separately in the manifest.
- Independent CPTAC data. Dou et al., Cancer Cell 2023, DOI 10.1016/j.ccell.2023.07.007, PMID 37567170. Exact version-3.0 source files and SHA-256 are in external_sources.json. The public release retrieves them from LinkedOmics rather than redistributing their molecular matrices and metadata. The adapter performs gene mapping, exclusions and the 138-case pairing, and has been tested against the local analysis inputs.
- MSigDB Hallmark gene sets. Liberzon et al., Cell Systems 2015, DOI 10.1016/j.cels.2015.12.004, PMID 26771021. h.all.v2025.1.Hs.symbols.gmt. Copyright Broad Institute, Massachusetts Institute of Technology and Regents of the University of California. The current MSigDB terms apply CC-BY-4.0 to versions 2022.1 and above. This release includes only the HALLMARK collection, not the separately restricted KEGG/BioCarta collections. Attribution and source naming are retained. https://www.gsea-msigdb.org/gsea/msigdb_license_terms.jsp
- HGNC. The exact frozen coding-gene/alias table is recorded by SHA-256. HGNC data are CC0, with attribution recommended. https://www.genenames.org/about/license/
- GEO processed data. GSE173682, GSE278879, GSE17025 and GSE63678 support the supplied lineage aggregates and array matrices. Regner et al., Mol Cell 2021, PMID 34739872, supports GSE173682 provenance. GEO states NCBI places no restrictions on use/distribution of GEO data; article text and other copyrighted material are distinct. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html. Upstream source papers and study-specific limitations remain applicable. Nuclear library labels do not establish unique donors.
- TCGA/cBioPortal clinical and RNA inputs. Liu et al., Cell 2018, DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2018.02.052, PMID 29625055, provides outcome reporting principles. Original TCGA and portal provenance are retained; no restricted-access sequence data are included. cBioPortal data are generally under ODbL with original-study attribution unless stated otherwise; derived database terms remain distinct from the code. https://docs.cbioportal.org/user-guide/faq/ and https://github.com/cBioPortal/datahub#license
- TCPA RPPA500 and author antibody mapping. Li et al., Nature Cancer 2024, DOI 10.1038/s43018-024-00817-x, PMID 39227745. The public archive retrieves the original matrix and Supplementary Tables workbook using optional_sources.json rather than redistributing the files. Their exact hashes and original URLs are required. Matching is by participant and primary-tumor type, not certified same aliquot.

Full-text articles used for citation checking, unpublished manuscript drafts, personal paths, credentials, author declarations and raw sequencing files are not included in the public software/data archive. This attribution record is not an institutional ethics determination.
